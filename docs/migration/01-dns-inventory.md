## 1. Authoritative DNS is at GoDaddy, not Cloudflare

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Nameservers | `ns65.domaincontrol.com` · `ns66.domaincontrol.com` |
| SOA primary | `ns65.domaincontrol.com` |
| SOA responsible | `dns.jomax.net` |

**This is a key finding.** The portability plan states Cloudflare should manage DNS for `gigitekai.com` and its subdomains. It does not today — **GoDaddy is authoritative**, and Cloudflare is only in front as a proxy. Moving the zone to Cloudflare (a nameserver change at the registrar) is a prerequisite for the target architecture, and it is a **separate operation from repointing the origin**.

> ⚠️ A nameserver change is the highest-blast-radius step in this migration. It must not happen until the `/admin` portal has been tested end to end on the new origin.

## 5. Findings worth acting on

### 5.1 The Manus origin dependency is explicit in DNS
Both `www` and `mainx` carry CNAMEs into `manus.space`. Repointing these to the independent origin is the actual cutover. Nothing else in DNS points at Manus.

### 5.2 Mail config is internally inconsistent — investigate before cutover
- **MX records point to Zoho** (`mx.zoho.com`)
- **`autodiscover` points to Microsoft** (`autodiscover.outlook.com`)

Those disagree. Autodiscover tells mail clients "find Exchange at Microsoft", while the mailboxes actually live at Zoho. That can cause Outlook/phone clients to fail autoconfiguration or look for a mailbox that isn't there. It may be a leftover from an earlier Microsoft 365 setup.

This is **not** caused by the migration, but it will surface during it. Worth confirming whether the Microsoft autodiscover record is still needed before the zone moves.

### 5.3 TTLs are already low on the records that matter
`www` and `mainx` are at **300s**, so rollback after a cutover would propagate quickly. The apex A is at 1800s — **lower it to 300s at least 24 hours before any apex change**.

### 5.4 `mail.gigitekai.com` has a malformed-looking MX
An MX entry with an empty preference/exchange was returned for `mail` and for `www`/`mainx`. This is likely a resolver artifact rather than a real record, but confirm it in the GoDaddy editor before migrating rather than copying it forward.

## 6. Cutover prerequisites derived from this inventory

1. Confirm the Microsoft `autodiscover` record is still required (see 5.2).
2. Lower the apex A-record TTL to 300s **24h before** the change.
3. Stand up the independent origin and verify it by hostname **before** touching DNS.
4. Move the zone to Cloudflare **only after** `/admin` is verified on the new origin.
5. Repoint `www` and `mainx` CNAMEs off `manus.space` last.
6. Keep Zoho MX/TXT records **byte-identical** throughout — mail must not be disturbed by a web migration.

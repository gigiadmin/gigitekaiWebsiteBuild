## Conversation Patterns for Regulated-Sector SMBs

### Opening (Discovery & Trust-Building)

1. **Acknowledge their world:** "I understand compliance and security are non-negotiable in [healthcare/legal/construction/finance]. One breach or audit failure can be catastrophic."
2. **Ask about their industry:** "What sector are you in? What compliance frameworks do you operate under?"
3. **Identify compliance pain points:** "What's your biggest compliance challenge right now—audit readiness, breach response, policy enforcement, or something else?"
4. **Gauge compliance maturity:** "How audit-ready are you today? Do you have a formal compliance program, or are you building it?"
5. **Understand their risk appetite:** "What keeps you up at night—regulatory fines, data breaches, audit failures?"

### Middle (Positioning as Compliance Partner)

1. **Validate their concerns:** "That's a common challenge in [your industry]. Here's how we've solved it for similar firms..."
2. **Map to service tier:** Based on their compliance maturity, recommend the right tier (Essential for basic setup, Professional for serious compliance, Elite for automation).
3. **Share industry-specific social proof:** "We've helped [X] healthcare practices pass HIPAA audits," or "We've helped [X] law firms achieve SOC 2 certification."
4. **Highlight compliance automation:** "Here's where AI agents make a difference—automating compliance workflows so your team focuses on core business."
5. **Address industry-specific objections:** "I hear that. Here's how we've maintained compliance while scaling for similar firms..."

### Closing (Next Steps & Compliance Assurance)

1. **Propose action:** "Let's schedule a 30-minute compliance discovery call with Harry to review your current setup and identify gaps."
2. **Set expectations:** "We'll audit your current infrastructure against [HIPAA/legal/CMMC/SOC 2] standards, identify quick wins, and outline a roadmap to full compliance."
3. **Emphasize partnership:** "We'll be your compliance partner—not just a vendor. You'll have direct access to our team."
4. **Confirm:** "Does Tuesday or Wednesday work better for you?"

## What NOT to Do

❌ **Don't downplay compliance:** "HIPAA compliance is pretty straightforward." (Say: "HIPAA compliance is complex—here's how we ensure you're audit-ready.")  
❌ **Don't use generic IT language:** "We'll secure your network." (Say: "We'll implement Zero Trust architecture and maintain HIPAA-compliant encryption.")  
❌ **Don't dismiss audit concerns:** "Audits aren't that big a deal." (Say: "Audits are critical—we'll ensure you pass with flying colors.")  
❌ **Don't commit to compliance timelines:** "We'll get you HIPAA-compliant in 2 weeks." (Say: "Let's assess your current state and build a realistic compliance roadmap.")  
❌ **Don't ignore breach risk:** "Breaches are rare." (Say: "Breaches happen—here's our incident response protocol.")  
❌ **Don't be pushy:** "You need to decide today." (Say: "Take your time; compliance is too important to rush.")  
❌ **Don't oversell AI automation:** "AI will handle all your compliance." (Say: "AI automates compliance workflows, but your team remains accountable.")

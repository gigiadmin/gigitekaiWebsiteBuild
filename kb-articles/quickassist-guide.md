# QuickAssist: Remote Assistance and Screen Sharing Guide

## Overview

QuickAssist is a Microsoft tool that enables secure, real-time remote assistance and screen sharing. This guide covers how to use QuickAssist for troubleshooting, training, and collaborative problem-solving within your organization.

## What is QuickAssist?

QuickAssist allows you to share your screen with a support technician or colleague who can view your desktop and, with your permission, take control to help resolve issues. All connections are encrypted and require explicit permission at each step, ensuring security and privacy.

**Key Benefits:**
- Instant remote access without complex setup
- Encrypted connections for security
- Works on Windows 10 and later
- No installation required (built into Windows)
- Session logging for compliance
- Works across networks and firewalls

## System Requirements

**Minimum Requirements:**
- Windows 10 or later (Home, Pro, Enterprise editions)
- Active internet connection
- Microsoft account (optional but recommended)
- Screen sharing permissions enabled

**Supported Browsers:**
- Microsoft Edge
- Google Chrome
- Firefox

**Network Requirements:**
- Outbound HTTPS access to quickassist.microsoft.com
- No VPN required (but compatible with VPN)
- Works through most firewalls and proxies

## Getting Started with QuickAssist

### Method 1: Using Windows Search

The quickest way to access QuickAssist is through Windows Search.

1. Click the **Windows Start** button or press the **Windows key**
2. Type **"Quick Assist"** in the search box
3. Click **Quick Assist** from the search results

2. Search for **"Quick Assist"**

You can also access QuickAssist through the web browser:

1. Open your web browser (Edge, Chrome, or Firefox)
2. Navigate to **https://quickassist.microsoft.com**
3. Sign in with your Microsoft account
4. Follow the on-screen instructions

## How to Request Remote Assistance

### Step 1: Open QuickAssist

Launch QuickAssist using one of the methods above. You will see two main options: "Get Assist" and "Give Assist."

### Step 2: Select "Get Assist"

Click the **"Get Assist"** button. This indicates you are requesting remote assistance from a technician or colleague.

### Step 3: Sign In (if prompted)

You may be prompted to sign in with your Microsoft account. This is optional but recommended for better tracking and security. If you prefer not to sign in, you can continue as a guest.

### Step 4: Share Your Session Code

Once you select "Get Assist," QuickAssist will generate a unique **session code** (typically 6-8 characters). This code is displayed prominently on your screen.

**Important:** Share this code only with the person you trust to provide assistance. The code is temporary and expires after a short period.

### Step 5: Grant Permission

When your support technician connects using the session code, you will see a prompt asking to confirm the connection. Review the technician's name or ID and click **"Allow"** to proceed.

### Step 6: Choose Permission Level

You have two options for what the technician can do:

**View Only:** The technician can see your screen but cannot interact with it. Use this for demonstrations or when you want to control the troubleshooting process.

**Take Control:** The technician can see your screen AND move your mouse and keyboard. This is the most efficient option for troubleshooting but should only be granted to trusted individuals.

Select your preferred option and click **"Continue"**.

### Step 7: Troubleshooting Session

Once connected, the technician can:
- See your entire screen in real-time
- Guide you through steps verbally or via chat
- Take control if you granted permission
- Share files or screenshots
- Annotate your screen to highlight issues

### Step 8: End the Session

When troubleshooting is complete, either you or the technician can end the session by clicking the **"Stop Sharing"** or **"End Session"** button. The connection will be immediately terminated.

## How to Provide Remote Assistance

### Step 1: Open QuickAssist

Launch QuickAssist on your computer.

### Step 2: Select "Give Assist"

Click the **"Give Assist"** button. This indicates you are ready to provide remote assistance.

### Step 3: Sign In

Sign in with your Microsoft account. This helps with session tracking and compliance logging.

### Step 4: Enter the Session Code

The person requesting assistance will provide you with a session code. Enter this code in the field provided and click **"Connect"**.

### Step 5: Wait for Approval

The person on the other end will see a prompt asking to confirm your connection. Wait for them to approve before proceeding.

### Step 6: Receive Permission Level

Once approved, you will see what permission level they have granted you (View Only or Take Control). Respect their choice and work within those boundaries.

### Step 7: Provide Assistance

Use QuickAssist to:
- View their screen and understand the issue
- Guide them through troubleshooting steps
- Take control if permitted to demonstrate solutions
- Use the annotation tools to highlight areas
- Share relevant documentation or links

### Step 8: Document the Session

After the session ends, document what was done:
- Note the issue and resolution
- Record the time spent
- Update the ticket or support record
- Follow up with the user if needed

## Best Practices for QuickAssist

### For Users Requesting Assistance

**Before the Session:**
- Close any sensitive applications or documents
- Close unnecessary browser tabs and windows
- Disable notifications to avoid distractions
- Have your issue description ready
- Ensure stable internet connection

**During the Session:**
- Stay at your computer during the session
- Ask questions if you don't understand the steps
- Don't grant "Take Control" unless absolutely necessary
- Watch what the technician does to learn
- Take notes on the solution for future reference

**After the Session:**
- Test that the issue is resolved
- Provide feedback on the support experience
- Keep the session transcript for your records
- Follow any post-session instructions provided

### For Technicians Providing Assistance

**Before Connecting:**
- Verify the session code with the user
- Confirm the issue description
- Have relevant documentation ready
- Ensure you have necessary permissions
- Test your audio/video if using voice chat

**During the Session:**
- Explain what you're doing as you work
- Respect the user's privacy and data
- Only access what's necessary to resolve the issue
- Take control only when needed
- Use annotation tools to highlight important areas

**After the Session:**
- Summarize what was done and why
- Provide next steps or preventive measures
- Document the resolution in the ticket system
- Follow up if the issue recurs
- Update the knowledge base if applicable

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: "QuickAssist is not available in your region"

**Solution:** QuickAssist may not be available in all regions. If you see this message, contact your IT support team for alternative remote assistance options.

### Issue: "Cannot connect to the session"

**Possible Causes:**
- Incorrect session code entered
- Session code has expired (codes expire after 10 minutes)
- Network connectivity issues
- Firewall blocking QuickAssist

**Solution:**
- Request a new session code from the user
- Check your internet connection
- Verify firewall allows quickassist.microsoft.com
- Try using the web version instead of the desktop app

### Issue: "Audio/Video not working"

**Solution:**
- Check microphone and speaker settings
- Verify permissions are granted for audio/video
- Restart QuickAssist
- Use text chat as an alternative
- Check Windows audio settings

### Issue: "Screen sharing is slow or laggy"

**Solution:**
- Close unnecessary applications
- Reduce screen resolution temporarily
- Move closer to your WiFi router
- Use wired Ethernet connection if possible
- Restart your computer

### Issue: "Permission denied when trying to take control"

**Solution:**
- The user has granted "View Only" permission
- Ask the user to end the session and reconnect with "Take Control" permission
- Or, guide the user through the steps verbally

## Security and Privacy Considerations

### Session Codes

- Session codes are temporary and unique to each session
- Codes expire after 10 minutes of inactivity
- Never share your session code publicly
- Only share with trusted individuals
- Each session requires explicit permission

### Encryption

- All QuickAssist connections use end-to-end encryption
- Data is not stored on Microsoft servers after the session ends
- Sessions are logged for compliance purposes
- Your screen content is not recorded by default

### Permissions

- You maintain full control over what the technician can see and do
- You can revoke permissions at any time by ending the session
- "Take Control" permission can be revoked mid-session
- You can close QuickAssist at any time

### Compliance

- Session logs are maintained for audit purposes
- Sensitive data should be minimized during sessions
- Follow your organization's data handling policies
- Report any suspicious activity to IT security

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is QuickAssist free?**
A: Yes, QuickAssist is free and included with Windows 10 and later.

**Q: Can QuickAssist be used on Mac or Linux?**
A: QuickAssist is Windows-only, but you can access the web version through a browser on other operating systems.

**Q: How long can a QuickAssist session last?**
A: Sessions can last as long as needed. There is no built-in time limit.

**Q: Can I use QuickAssist on a mobile device?**
A: QuickAssist is designed for desktop and laptop computers. Mobile support is limited.

**Q: Is my data safe during a QuickAssist session?**
A: Yes, all connections are encrypted and you maintain full control over permissions.

**Q: What if I accidentally grant "Take Control" permission?**
A: You can revoke it immediately by clicking "Take View Only" or ending the session.

**Q: Can I record a QuickAssist session?**
A: Recording is not built into QuickAssist, but you can use screen recording software if needed.

**Q: What if the technician disconnects unexpectedly?**
A: The session will end and you'll need to request a new session code to reconnect.

# Peripheral Devices Troubleshooting Guide

**Category:** Hardware & Peripherals | **Tier:** 1 | **Resolution Time:** 5-15 minutes | **Difficulty:** Beginner

## Overview

Peripheral devices like keyboards, mice, monitors, docking stations, and USB devices are essential for productivity. This guide covers setup, troubleshooting, and solutions for common peripheral issues.

## Common Peripheral Issues

| Device | Issue | Typical Cause |
|--------|-------|---------------|
| Keyboard | Keys not responding or stuck | Disconnected, driver issue, or physical damage |
| Mouse | Cursor not moving or laggy | Disconnected, low battery, or driver issue |
| Monitor | No display or flickering | Disconnected, wrong input, or driver issue |
| Docking Station | Devices not recognized | Disconnected, driver issue, or firmware outdated |
| USB Device | Not recognized or not working | Disconnected, driver missing, or port issue |
| Headphones | No sound or microphone not working | Disconnected, muted, or driver issue |
| Webcam | Not detected or poor video quality | Disconnected, driver issue, or permission denied |
| Charger | Device not charging | Disconnected, cable damaged, or port issue |

## Solution Steps

### Step 1: Check Physical Connections

Before troubleshooting, verify all connections:

1. **Verify device is powered on** - Check power button or battery indicator
2. **Check cable connections** - Ensure cables are firmly plugged in
3. **Look for LED indicators** - Should show device is powered and connected
4. **Try different USB port** - If USB device, try another port on your computer
5. **Check for damage** - Look for bent pins, broken connectors, or physical damage
6. **Inspect cables** - Look for cuts, fraying, or visible damage

### Step 2: Restart Your Device

A simple restart often resolves peripheral issues:

1. **Disconnect the peripheral** - Unplug USB cable or turn off wireless device
2. **Restart your computer** - Windows: Start menu → Power → Restart | Mac: Apple menu → Restart
3. **Wait for computer to fully boot** - Usually 1-2 minutes
4. **Reconnect the peripheral** - Plug in USB or turn on wireless device
5. **Wait for recognition** - Computer should detect the device automatically
6. **Test the device** - Try using it to verify it works

### Step 3: Reconnect Wireless Devices

For wireless keyboards, mice, and headphones:

1. **Check battery level** - Replace batteries if low (usually indicated by LED)
2. **Turn device off and on** - Power button should reset connection
3. **Re-pair the device:**
   - **Windows:** Settings → Devices → Bluetooth & other devices → Add Bluetooth or other device
   - **Mac:** System Preferences → Bluetooth → Click "+" to add device
4. **Select your device** - Find it in the list and click "Connect"
5. **Wait for pairing** - Usually takes 10-30 seconds
6. **Test the device** - Verify it's working

### Step 4: Update Device Drivers

Outdated drivers can cause peripheral issues:

**For Windows:**
1. Right-click the Start menu → "Device Manager"
2. Expand the device category (e.g., "Keyboards", "Mice and other pointing devices")
3. Right-click your device → "Update driver"
4. Select "Search automatically for updated driver software"
5. Follow prompts and restart if asked
6. Test the device

**For Mac:**
- Mac typically updates automatically
- Check System Preferences → Software Update

### Step 5: Troubleshoot Keyboard Issues

If keyboard isn't responding:

1. **Check Num Lock** - Press Num Lock key; indicator light should toggle
2. **Try different keys** - Verify multiple keys aren't responding
3. **Clean the keyboard** - Remove dust and debris
4. **Check for stuck keys** - Gently press each key to ensure it moves freely
5. **Disconnect and reconnect** - Unplug USB or re-pair wireless
6. **Update keyboard driver** - Follow Step 4 above
7. **Replace keyboard** - If none of above work, keyboard may be damaged

### Step 6: Troubleshoot Mouse Issues

If mouse cursor isn't moving or is laggy:

1. **Check mouse battery** - Replace batteries if wireless mouse is sluggish
2. **Clean mouse sensor** - Wipe the bottom with a dry cloth
3. **Adjust mouse sensitivity:**
   - **Windows:** Settings → Devices → Mouse → Adjust cursor speed
   - **Mac:** System Preferences → Accessibility → Pointer Control
4. **Disconnect and reconnect** - Unplug USB or re-pair wireless
5. **Update mouse driver** - Follow Step 4 above
6. **Try different surface** - Mouse pad or surface may be affecting sensor
7. **Replace mouse** - If none of above work, mouse may be defective

### Step 7: Troubleshoot Monitor Issues

If monitor shows no display or flickering:

1. **Check power** - Ensure monitor is powered on (check power button and LED)
2. **Check cable connections** - Verify video cable is firmly connected to both monitor and computer
3. **Try different video input:**
   - Press "Input" or "Source" button on monitor
   - Select different input (HDMI, DisplayPort, VGA)
4. **Adjust brightness and contrast** - Use monitor buttons to increase brightness
5. **Restart computer** - May fix display driver issue
6. **Update display driver** - Follow Step 4 above
7. **Try different cable** - Video cable may be damaged
8. **Replace monitor** - If none of above work, monitor may be defective

### Step 8: Troubleshoot Docking Station Issues

If docking station isn't recognizing devices:

1. **Check power** - Ensure docking station is plugged in and powered on
2. **Check USB connection** - Verify docking station is connected to your computer
3. **Restart docking station** - Unplug for 30 seconds, then plug back in
4. **Reconnect peripherals** - Unplug all devices from docking station, then reconnect
5. **Update docking station driver:**
   - Visit manufacturer's website
   - Download latest driver for your model
   - Install and restart computer
6. **Update docking station firmware** - Check manufacturer's website for firmware updates
7. **Replace docking station** - If none of above work, docking station may be defective

### Step 9: Troubleshoot USB Device Issues

If USB device isn't recognized:

1. **Try different USB port** - USB device may not work in certain ports
2. **Try different computer** - Test on another device to verify USB device works
3. **Check for driver** - Some USB devices require drivers
   - Visit manufacturer's website
   - Download and install latest drivers
   - Restart computer
4. **Disable USB Selective Suspend:**
   - **Windows:** Device Manager → Universal Serial Bus controllers → Right-click "USB Root Hub" → Properties → Power Management → Uncheck "Allow computer to turn off this device"
5. **Update USB drivers:**
   - Device Manager → Universal Serial Bus controllers → Right-click each item → Update driver
6. **Replace USB cable** - Cable may be damaged
7. **Replace USB device** - If none of above work, device may be defective

### Step 10: Troubleshoot Audio Issues

If headphones or speakers aren't working:

1. **Check volume** - Ensure volume is not muted (check speaker icon in system tray)
2. **Verify device is selected:**
   - **Windows:** Settings → System → Sound → Choose your output device
   - **Mac:** System Preferences → Sound → Output → Select your device
3. **Check connections** - Ensure headphones/speakers are firmly connected
4. **Restart audio service:**
   - **Windows:** Right-click Start → Device Manager → Sound, video and game controllers → Right-click audio device → Restart
5. **Update audio driver** - Follow Step 4 above
6. **Test with different device** - Verify audio works with another device
7. **Replace audio device** - If none of above work, device may be defective

## When to Escalate

Contact your IT support team if:

- Peripheral device is physically damaged
- Device won't work after trying all troubleshooting steps
- You need to set up specialized peripherals (scanners, label printers)
- Multiple peripherals stopped working simultaneously
- You need driver installation assistance
- Docking station firmware needs updating
- You need to replace a peripheral device

## Prevention Tips

**Maintain Peripheral Health:**
- Keep devices clean and dust-free
- Avoid excessive force when pressing keys or buttons
- Replace batteries in wireless devices before they're completely dead
- Store devices in safe location when not in use
- Use cable organizers to prevent cable damage
- Keep devices away from liquids and extreme temperatures

**Optimize Performance:**
- Update device drivers regularly
- Keep wireless devices within 30 feet of receiver
- Avoid interference from other wireless devices
- Use quality USB cables and hubs
- Don't overload USB ports with too many devices

## FAQ

**Q: How do I know if my peripheral device is broken?**
A: If device doesn't work after trying all troubleshooting steps, and works on another computer, the device is likely broken.

**Q: Can I use any keyboard or mouse with my computer?**
A: Most keyboards and mice are compatible with Windows and Mac. Check product specifications to verify compatibility.

**Q: How often should I replace peripheral devices?**
A: Quality peripherals last 3-5 years. Replace if damaged or no longer functioning.

**Q: Can I use multiple monitors with my computer?**
A: Yes, if your computer has multiple video outputs or a docking station. Contact IT for setup assistance.

**Q: What's the difference between USB 2.0 and USB 3.0?**
A: USB 3.0 is faster. Most modern devices use USB 3.0. Check device specifications for compatibility.

**Q: Can I use a wireless keyboard and mouse together?**
A: Yes, most wireless keyboards and mice use the same receiver and can be paired together.

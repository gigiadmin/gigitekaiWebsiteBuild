## Solution Overview (1 minute)

We've redesigned our pricing page with **four key features** that make it easy for prospects to find their perfect plan:

### 1. **Monthly/Annual Toggle with 10% Discount**
Customers can instantly see the cost difference between monthly and annual billing. When they toggle to annual, they see a **10% discount** applied automatically—showing them exactly how much they save by committing upfront.

### 2. **Dynamic User Count Calculator**
Instead of assuming a minimum 10-user deployment, prospects can enter their actual user count (10–1000 users) and see the **real total cost** for their organization. This removes friction and builds confidence.

### 3. **Volume Discount Tiers**
- **25–50 users:** 8% discount
- **50+ users:** Custom pricing (with "Request a Quote" button)

Volume discounts are clearly displayed, so larger organizations immediately see the value of scaling with us.

### 4. **Professional Plan Highlighted as "Most Popular"**
The Professional plan ($119/user/month) is visually emphasized with a **gradient cyan ribbon**, enhanced border, and feature icons. This guides customers toward our recommended tier while respecting their choice to pick Essential or Elite.

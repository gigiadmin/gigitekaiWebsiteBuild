## Speaker Tips

- **Emphasize the calculator:** It's the hero feature. Spend 1–2 minutes walking through a live demo if possible.
- **Use real numbers:** Pricing examples make the value concrete. Don't be shy about showing savings.
- **Highlight the Professional plan:** It's our sweet spot—most customers choose this tier.
- **Address objections early:** Mention "no long-term contracts" and "30-day cancellation" to reduce buying friction.
- **End with urgency:** "Visit the page today" creates a gentle call to action without being pushy.
- **Be ready to customize:** If presenting to a specific industry (healthcare, finance, etc.), be ready to discuss compliance features (HIPAA, SOC2, etc.).

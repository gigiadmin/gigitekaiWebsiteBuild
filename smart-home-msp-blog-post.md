## Why MSPs Should Enter Smart Home & Automation

### 1. **You Already Have the Trust**

Your clients trust you with their most critical systems—their email, their data, their security. You've proven you can:
- Manage complex infrastructure
- Provide 24/7 support
- Maintain compliance and security standards
- Deliver measurable ROI

Smart home automation is a natural extension of that trust. You're not asking them to work with a new vendor—you're offering them an integrated solution from someone they already rely on.

### 2. **Enterprise Smart Home is Different from Consumer**

When people think "smart home," they imagine Alexa and Ring doorbells. But enterprise smart home is fundamentally different:

**Enterprise Smart Home includes:**
- HVAC optimization and energy management
- Occupancy-based lighting and climate control
- Access control and security integration
- Meeting room automation
- Parking and fleet management
- Real estate utilization analytics

This isn't about convenience. This is about **reducing operational costs by 20-40%, improving employee productivity, and optimizing real estate spend.**

A 500-person enterprise can save **$200,000-$500,000 annually** through intelligent building automation. That's a compelling ROI story.

### 3. **Integration is Your Superpower**

Smart home systems don't exist in isolation. They need to integrate with:
- Microsoft 365 and Teams
- Azure IoT Hub
- Salesforce and CRM systems
- ERP platforms
- Security systems
- Network infrastructure

Most smart home vendors can't do this. They're specialists in their narrow domain. But **MSPs have the systems integration expertise** to connect these dots.

You can position yourself as the **orchestrator of intelligent infrastructure**—the partner who makes all these systems work together seamlessly.

### 4. **Recurring Revenue & Sticky Customers**

Smart home systems require:
- Ongoing monitoring and optimization
- Regular updates and patches
- Integration with new business tools
- Expansion as the organization grows

This creates **predictable, recurring revenue** and makes your customers stickier. Once you're managing their smart building infrastructure, they're not going anywhere.

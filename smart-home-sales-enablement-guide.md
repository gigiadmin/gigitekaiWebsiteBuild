## Discovery Questions

### Phase 1: Qualifying Questions (Initial Call)

1. **Building Profile**
   - How many buildings do you operate?
   - What's your total square footage?
   - What's your primary industry?

2. **Current State**
   - What systems are currently managing your building (HVAC, lighting, etc.)?
   - How much are you spending annually on energy?
   - What's your biggest operational pain point?

3. **Business Drivers**
   - What's your biggest priority: cost reduction, employee experience, or sustainability?
   - Do you have ESG or carbon reduction commitments?
   - What's your typical payback period requirement?

4. **Decision Process**
   - Who else needs to be involved in this decision?
   - What's your budget range?
   - What's your timeline?

### Phase 2: Deep Dive Questions (Discovery Call)

1. **Energy & Operations**
   - What's your current energy consumption (kWh/year)?
   - What percentage of that is HVAC vs. lighting vs. other?
   - How many facilities staff do you have?
   - What percentage of their time is reactive vs. preventive maintenance?

2. **Real Estate**
   - How much real estate are you currently leasing?
   - What's your occupancy rate by floor/building?
   - Are you looking to optimize space utilization?
   - Do you have plans to expand or consolidate?

3. **Technology**
   - What's your current tech stack? (M365, Azure, AWS, etc.)
   - How mature is your IT infrastructure?
   - What's your appetite for AI/automation?
   - Who manages building systems today?

4. **Business Goals**
   - What are your top 3 business priorities for the next 12 months?
   - How does operational efficiency fit into those priorities?
   - What would success look like?
   - What's your decision timeline?

## ROI Calculator Framework

### Energy Savings Model

```
Annual Energy Cost = (Current kWh/year × $/kWh) + Demand Charges

Smart Building Savings = Annual Energy Cost × 18-28% (typical range)

Example:
- Building: 100,000 sq ft
- Current energy cost: $180,000/year
- Smart building savings: 22% = $39,600/year
```

### Labor Savings Model

```
Annual Labor Savings = (Maintenance Hours Reduced × Hourly Rate) + (Emergency Call Reduction × Cost per Call)

Example:
- Current maintenance: 480 hours/year
- Reduction: 40% = 192 hours/year
- Hourly rate: $75
- Labor savings: $14,400/year
- Emergency calls reduced: 20/year × $500 = $10,000/year
- Total labor savings: $24,400/year
```

### Real Estate Optimization Model

```
Real Estate Savings = (Space Optimized sq ft × $/sq ft/year)

Example:
- Current occupancy: 65%
- Optimized occupancy: 85%
- Space that can be eliminated: 20,000 sq ft
- Cost per sq ft: $50/year
- Real estate savings: $1,000,000/year
```

### Total ROI Calculation

```
Total Annual Savings = Energy + Labor + Real Estate + Other

Setup Cost = Equipment + Installation + Integration + Training

Payback Period = Setup Cost / Total Annual Savings (in months)

ROI Year 1 = (Total Annual Savings - Monthly Managed Service Cost × 12) / Setup Cost

Example:
- Total Annual Savings: $64,000
- Setup Cost: $75,000
- Monthly Managed Service: $3,500
- Annual Service Cost: $42,000
- Net Year 1 Savings: $22,000
- Payback Period: 42 months (3.5 years) ← NOT GOOD
- Adjust scope to improve ROI

Better Example (with real estate savings):
- Total Annual Savings: $200,000
- Setup Cost: $75,000
- Monthly Managed Service: $5,000
- Annual Service Cost: $60,000
- Net Year 1 Savings: $140,000
- Payback Period: 6.4 months ← GOOD
- ROI Year 1: 187%
```

## Objection Handling

### Objection 1: "We're not ready for this yet"

**Response:**
"I understand. Most organizations aren't until they see the numbers. Let me ask: if I could show you a way to save $100K/year with a 6-month payback, would that change your timeline?"

**Follow-up:**
"Let's do a quick energy audit. No obligation. It'll take 2 hours and you'll have concrete data on what's possible."

---

### Objection 2: "We already have smart building systems"

**Response:**
"Great! That's a good foundation. Most organizations have point solutions—smart lighting here, smart HVAC there. The challenge is they don't talk to each other. What we do is integrate them into one unified system with AI optimization across the board."

**Follow-up:**
"Can I ask: are those systems integrated with your Microsoft 365 and Azure infrastructure? Are they AI-optimized? Do you have one support team managing them?"

---

### Objection 3: "This is too expensive"

**Response:**
"I hear that. Most organizations think the same thing until they see the ROI. Let me ask: what's your current annual energy spend?"

[Listen to answer]

"Typical savings are 18-28% of that. So if you're spending $200K/year on energy, that's $36K-$56K in annual savings. Our system typically pays for itself in 12-18 months. After that, it's pure profit."

**Follow-up:**
"Let's model out the specific ROI for your building. I think you'll be surprised."

---

### Objection 4: "We're concerned about security"

**Response:**
"That's the right concern. Enterprise security is non-negotiable. We're SOC 2 compliant, HIPAA ready, and PCI-DSS certified. All data is encrypted end-to-end. Everything integrates with your existing security infrastructure."

**Follow-up:**
"Let me walk you through our security architecture. I think you'll see it meets or exceeds your standards."

---

### Objection 5: "We need to think about it"

**Response:**
"Absolutely. This is an important decision. Let me ask: what would help you make this decision? Is it more information about ROI? A reference call with another client? A pilot project?"

**Follow-up:**

## Deal Stages & Playbook

### Stage 1: Awareness (Inbound Lead or Outbound Prospecting)

**Objective:** Qualify the opportunity and schedule discovery call

**Activities:**
- Initial qualification call (15 min)
- Ask discovery questions (Phase 1)
- Determine fit and budget
- Schedule discovery call

**Success Criteria:**
- Qualified lead (fits our ideal customer profile)
- Budget identified ($50K+ setup cost)
- Timeline identified (6-12 month decision)
- Next meeting scheduled

---

### Stage 2: Discovery (Deep Dive Call)

**Objective:** Understand their business drivers and build business case

**Activities:**
- Ask discovery questions (Phase 2)
- Walk through energy audit process
- Discuss integration requirements
- Introduce team members (Solution Architect, Account Manager)

**Deliverable:**
- Energy audit proposal (if appropriate)

**Success Criteria:**
- Clear understanding of their pain points
- Budget confirmed
- Decision timeline confirmed
- Next step agreed (energy audit or proposal)

---

### Stage 3: Assessment (Energy Audit & ROI Modeling)

**Objective:** Build concrete ROI model and business case

**Activities:**
- Conduct on-site energy audit (4-8 hours)
- Analyze current systems and operations
- Model energy, labor, and real estate savings
- Present findings and ROI model

**Deliverable:**
- Smart Building Strategy Document
- ROI Model (3-year projection)
- Implementation timeline
- Pricing proposal

**Success Criteria:**
- ROI model shows 6-18 month payback
- All stakeholders have seen the business case
- Proposal accepted or minor revisions requested
- Next step agreed (contract negotiation or pilot)

---

### Stage 4: Proposal & Negotiation

**Objective:** Finalize contract and implementation plan

**Activities:**
- Present proposal to decision-makers
- Address questions and concerns
- Negotiate terms and pricing
- Finalize statement of work

**Deliverable:**
- Signed contract
- Detailed implementation plan
- Project timeline
- Support SLA

**Success Criteria:**
- Contract signed
- Implementation team assigned
- Kickoff meeting scheduled
- Project timeline confirmed

---

### Stage 5: Implementation

**Objective:** Deploy smart building system and achieve ROI

**Activities:**
- Install IoT sensors and equipment
- Integrate with existing systems
- Configure AI optimization
- Train staff
- Go live

**Deliverable:**
- Live smart building system
- Trained staff
- Documentation and runbooks
- Baseline metrics established

**Success Criteria:**
- System live and operational
- Staff trained and confident
- Baseline metrics captured
- Support handoff complete

---

### Stage 6: Optimization & Expansion

**Objective:** Achieve promised ROI and identify expansion opportunities

**Activities:**
- Monitor system performance
- Conduct monthly optimization reviews
- Track ROI against projections
- Identify expansion opportunities (additional buildings, new use cases)

**Deliverable:**
- Monthly performance reports
- Quarterly business reviews
- Expansion proposals

**Success Criteria:**
- ROI on track or exceeding projections
- Customer satisfaction high
- Expansion opportunities identified
- Renewal/expansion deal in pipeline
